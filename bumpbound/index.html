Questions

FAQ

Is Bumpbound free?

Yes. The game is free to play. It has ads and optional in-app purchases: coin packs, Remove Ads and premium car finishes. Rewarded ads are always optional.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. Career, the Football Cup and quick matches against bots all work offline. You only need internet for online matches.

How do I play with a friend?

Open Online, host a private room and share the room code. Your friend enters the code to join.

Can I buy an advantage online?

No. Cosmetics only change how your car looks. In online matches every car uses the same stats. Performance upgrades apply to offline play.

I bought something on a new phone. How do I get it back?

Sign in with the same store account and use Restore Purchases in the Store.
